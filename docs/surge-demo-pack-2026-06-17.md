# Surge Demo Pack - 17 Jun 2026

## Aim

Show Surge as useful today without overselling it.

Core line:

> Surge today is AMPD's sales-engineering assistant. It helps turn messy temporary-power questions into a fast, useful first-pass answer: what Ampd unit is likely to fit, what assumptions matter, and what commercial story we can tell. The bigger opportunity is widening it into a standalone platform for project assessment, proposal generation and live evidence-backed deployment planning.

## 5-Minute Demo Flow

### 1. Set the frame - 30 seconds

Say:

> I don't want to show this as a generic chatbot. The useful bit is that it understands the AMPD sales-engineering workflow: plant loads, generator baselines, Ampd 200 / 400 recommendations, recharge assumptions, diesel saving, CO2 saving and customer-friendly caveats.

Then:

> Today it's a focused assistant. The product opportunity is making it a repeatable platform.

### 2. Simple plant/load screen - 90 seconds

Use a clean prompt, not the full Dublin job.

Prompt:

> We have two tower cranes at 160 kVA each on a 52-week project. Recharge is from a diesel generator. Can Ampd support this and what would you recommend?

What to look for:
- It gives a useful first-pass screen.
- It separates assumptions from confidence.
- It asks for missing detail rather than pretending it knows everything.
- It positions Ampd 200 / Ampd 400 sensibly.

If asked why it assumes duration/recharge:

> That was a deliberate improvement. Sales conversations often start incomplete. Surge should be useful first, then caveat and ask for the missing data.

### 3. Commercial comparison - 90 seconds

Prompt:

> Compare the likely diesel generator baseline against an Ampd-supported option. Keep it customer-friendly and highlight what assumptions need confirming.

What to look for:
- Generator displacement narrative.
- Cost / fuel / CO2 framing.
- Clear assumption language.
- No fake certainty.

Say:

> This is where it helps sales. It gets us from vague site information to a defensible conversation quickly.

### 4. Proposal-style output - 90 seconds

Prompt:

> Turn that into a short customer email explaining the recommendation, assumptions, and next information needed.

What to look for:
- Clean customer wording.
- No overloaded technical detail.
- One useful next question.

Say:

> The value is consistency. Every sales person can get to a decent first-pass answer and a clear next ask, instead of waiting for technical back-and-forth.

### 5. Bridge to standalone platform - 60 seconds

Say:

> The bigger product is not just Surge answering messages. The bigger product is a project workspace: upload a programme, plant list, drawings, telemetry and field evidence, then generate assessments, proposals and live operating proof from one place.

Then use this line:

> Surge today is the assistant. Standalone Surge is the operating system for temporary-power assessment and deployment planning.

## Three Safe Demo Prompts

### Prompt 1 - quick screening

> We have two tower cranes at 160 kVA each on a 52-week project. Recharge is from a diesel generator. Can Ampd support this and what would you recommend?

### Prompt 2 - messy sales input

> Customer says they have a tower crane, hoist, welfare cabins and general site power. They do not know the actual load profile yet. What should we ask for, and can we give a first-pass Ampd view?

### Prompt 3 - proposal output

> Write a short customer-facing recommendation for an Ampd-supported temporary-power setup. Include the recommendation, assumptions, commercial benefits and the one most important missing data point.

## What Surge Can Do Now

- Screen plant/load scenarios for Ampd suitability.
- Compare diesel generator baseline vs Ampd options.
- Recommend Ampd 200 / Ampd 400 where inputs support it.
- Ask sensible missing-data questions.
- Use mains vs generator recharge assumptions.
- Produce quick cost, fuel, CO2 and commercial framing.
- Turn technical reasoning into customer-friendly copy.
- Support consistent sales qualification and proposal conversations.

## What Not To Oversell Yet

Do not claim Surge fully does these unaided today:

- Ingests a whole complex project from raw drawings/programmes on its own.
- Automatically builds deployment phasing for multi-block sites.
- Replaces technical review for major projects.
- Has complete field evidence for every plant type.
- Knows unknown project constraints unless provided.

Safe wording:

> Surge can calculate and present from structured assumptions today. The standalone product would make those assumptions easier to capture, track, reuse and evidence.

## Standalone Platform Direction

The sellable product:

> Surge: a platform for temporary-power assessment, proposal generation and evidence-backed deployment planning.

Modules:
- Project workspace: customers, sites, plant lists, programme dates, drawings, saved assessments.
- Evidence layer: manufacturer data, customer inputs, field evidence, live telemetry, assumptions clearly labelled.
- Assessment engine: Ampd sizing, generator baseline, recharge strategy, missing-data questions.
- Commercial engine: diesel saving, CO2 saving, rental/purchase economics, partner/channel cases.
- Proposal engine: customer emails, PDF packs, assumption sheets, technical/commercial summaries.
- Monitoring intelligence: SOC/load/runtime evidence, alert history, fleet behaviour, proof for future proposals.

CEO-level line:

> This sits close to revenue. It speeds up qualification, improves proposal quality, reduces technical bottlenecks, and turns field evidence into reusable commercial proof.

## If Anthony Asks "Is This A Product?"

Answer:

> Yes, if we widen it carefully. Today it is a focused assistant that proves the workflow. The product is the repeatable system around it: project workspace, assessment engine, proposal engine and live evidence layer. That's where it becomes sellable beyond an internal helper.

## If Chrystin Asks "What Is The Immediate Value?"

Answer:

> Faster qualification and better consistency. Surge helps sales get to a useful first-pass recommendation, identify the missing information, and produce cleaner customer communication without waiting for every question to become a technical deep dive.

## If They Ask About The Dublin / St Vincent's Type Job

Answer:

> Surge can support that type of work when the assumptions are structured. For a job like Dublin, the real value is a project workspace: programme dates, crane schedule, block mapping, phasing, recharge source and proposal narrative. Today I would not say Surge ingests and solves that whole job unaided, but it can calculate and present the assessment once the project context is organised.

## Risks To Keep Honest

- Separate measured field evidence from assumptions.
- Do not present first-pass screening as proof.
- Do not use competitor comparisons in AMPD-facing conversation.
- Do not let the demo become a giant technical assessment.
- Keep it commercial: speed, consistency, confidence, evidence reuse.

## Suggested Close

> My view is we keep Surge focused for AMPD now, because that makes the demo credible. But the bigger opportunity is clear: turn the sales-engineering judgement into a platform that captures project inputs, creates proposals, and learns from live deployments.
