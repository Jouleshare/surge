## What data we collect

When you message Surge on WhatsApp, we collect:

- Your **WhatsApp phone number** (provided automatically by WhatsApp)
- Your **name** (if shared during the conversation)
- The **content of your messages** (your questions and our replies)
- Your **email address** (only if you voluntarily provide it when requesting more information)
- Your **company name** (only if you voluntarily provide it)

We do **not** collect payment information, location data, or any sensitive personal data.

## Why we collect it and our lawful basis

| Data | Purpose | Lawful Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number, messages | Providing the Surge service | Legitimate interests |
| Name, email, company | Following up on your enquiry | Legitimate interests |
| Conversation history | Improving the quality of responses | Legitimate interests |

Our legitimate interest is providing you with the service you initiated by messaging us, and following up on any power calculation enquiry you have made.

## How long we keep your data

- **Conversation history:** 2 years from your last message
- **Lead contact details (name, email, company):** 2 years from last contact
- After the retention period, data is permanently deleted from our systems

---

## Who we share data with

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share it only with:

- **Meta Platforms (WhatsApp):** Your messages are transmitted via the WhatsApp Business API. Meta's privacy policy applies to the WhatsApp platform itself.
- **Anthropic / AI providers:** Conversation content may be processed by AI services to generate responses. No personal contact details are shared.
- **Resend (email service):** If you provide an email, your contact details are used to send a follow-up email. Resend processes this as our sub-processor.

All sub-processors are contractually bound to process data only on our instructions and in accordance with UK GDPR.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction** — ask us to limit how we use your data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Object** — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, email **tom@getjoule.co.uk** with the subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 30 days.
